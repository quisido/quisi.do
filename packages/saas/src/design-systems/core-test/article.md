- The component exposes an `article` role for independently meaningful
  content[^1].
- An article can receive an accessible name from its heading or from an external
  label[^1].
- A focusable article participates in keyboard navigation, including when it is
  used to expose reading content inside an application[^1].
- Nested articles retain distinct accessible names so information associated
  with an ancestor article is not incorrectly applied to its descendants[^1].

[^1]: https://w3c.github.io/aria/#article
