- The component exposes an `application` role with an accessible name[^1].
- Static content inside the application is associated with a focusable element,
  contained by a focusable `article` or `document`, or exposed through managed
  descendant focus[^1].
- Focusable `article` and `document` descendants let assistive-technology users
  return from application mode to reading mode[^1].
- A role description, accessible description, banner, and content information
  remain programmatically available when provided[^1].

[^1]: https://w3c.github.io/aria/#application
