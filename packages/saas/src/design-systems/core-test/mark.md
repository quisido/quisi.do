- The component exposes content marked for reference or notation through the
  `mark` role and preserves it in its surrounding reading context[^1].
- Marked content can identify information relevant to the user's current
  activity, such as a search match[^1].
- Marked content is not made interactive or focusable merely because it is
  highlighted[^1].
