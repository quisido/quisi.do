- The component exposes author-provided supplemental or parenthetical content
  through the `note` role[^1].
- A note in the normal content flow retains its reading-order association with
  the primary content it supplements[^1].
- Brief static note text can be associated with primary content through
  `aria-describedby`[^1].
- Structured or interactive note content can be associated with primary
  content through `aria-details` without making the note itself
  interactive[^1].

[^1]: https://w3c.github.io/aria/#note
