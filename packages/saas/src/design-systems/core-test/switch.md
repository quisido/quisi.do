- The component exposes an on/off control through the `switch` role with an
  accessible name[^1].
- `aria-checked="true"` represents on and `aria-checked="false"` represents
  off; the `mixed` value is invalid for a switch and is treated as false[^1].
- Pointer activation and `Space` toggle the switch, and the switch participates
  in the page `Tab` sequence[^2].
