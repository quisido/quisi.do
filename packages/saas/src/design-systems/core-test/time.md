- The text contents or `datetime` attribute must be a valid date- or
  time-related string[^1].
- Valid strings[^1]:
  - Month: 2019-11
  - Date: 2019-11-18
  - Yearless date: 11-18
  - Time: 09:54:39
  - Floating date and time: 2019-11-18T14:54
  - Time-zone offset: -08:00
  - Global date and time: 2019-11-18T14:54Z
  - Week: 2019-W47
  - Four or more ASCII digits, at least one of which is not U+0030 DIGIT ZERO (0): 0001
  - Duration: 4h 18m 3s

[^1]: https://w3c.github.io/aria/#time
