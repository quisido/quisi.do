- A suggestion has either exactly one insertion accessibility child or exactly
  one deletion accessibility child, or exactly two accessibility children where
  one is an insertion and the other is a deletion[^1].
- Do not include any additional accessibility children in a suggestion[^1].
- Use `aria-details` or `aria-description` to associate the suggestion with
  related information such as comments, authoring info, and time stamps[^1].

[^1]: https://w3c.github.io/aria/#suggestion
