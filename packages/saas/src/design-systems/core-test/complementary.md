- The component exposes supporting content as a `complementary` landmark[^1].
- The landmark is a sibling or direct descendant of the main content it
  complements[^1].
- Its content remains meaningful when separated from the main content[^1].
- Multiple complementary landmarks have distinct accessible names[^2].

[^1]: https://w3c.github.io/aria/#complementary

[^2]: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/landmarks/examples/complementary.html
