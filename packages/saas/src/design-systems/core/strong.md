# Strong

Strong content communicates strong importance, seriousness, or urgency. It is
not for communicating changes in typographical presentation that are not
important to the meaning of the content. Use strong content only if its absence
would change the meaning of the content.

Strong content is not intended to convey stress or emphasis; for that purpose,
Emphasis component is more appropriate.
